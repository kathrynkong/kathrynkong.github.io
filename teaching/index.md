# Teaching

## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching connects critical inquiry with hands-on engagement in digital media, games, AI, and language learning. Across university classrooms, multilingual learning environments, and public conservation education, I help learners connect unfamiliar ideas to their own experiences.

I adapt explanations and activities to learners' language proficiency, cultural backgrounds, and learning needs. Practical communication, active participation, individualized feedback, and formative assessment guide my approach. I also encourage learners to question how digital systems shape communication, culture, and creativity.

## University Teaching

### Instructor, Introduction to Digital Media

**University of Central Florida · Nicholson School of Communication and Media**  
Orlando, Florida · **August 2026–present**

I teach an undergraduate introductory course examining the historical development, foundational concepts, and social implications of digital media and computing. I guide students in critically evaluating emerging technologies and media practices and assess their learning outcomes.

Course topics include analog and digital media, compression, copyright, social media, video games, immersive media, and generative AI.

Shanghai, China, and Seoul, South Korea · **2019–2021**

## Critical AI Literacy and Curriculum Development

In summer 2026, I participated in **Building a Digital Humanities Generative AI Learning Community** at UCF, led by Dr. Anastasia Salter and Dr. Mel Stanfill and funded by the National Endowment for the Humanities.

The biweekly community focused on integrating critical AI literacy into the Digital Humanities minor curriculum. I contributed to course redesign or development involving generative AI, machine-learning tools, and discussions of copyright, labor, environmental impact, and ethics.
