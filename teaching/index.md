# Teaching

## Teaching Philosophy

My teaching connects critical inquiry with hands-on engagement in digital media, games, AI, and language learning. I use familiar technologies and media examples to help students connect theory to everyday experience while encouraging them to question how digital systems shape communication, culture, and creativity.

## Introduction to Digital Media

I teach an undergraduate Introduction to Digital Media course at UCF covering the history, concepts, and social implications of digital media and computing. Topics include analog and digital media, compression, copyright, social media, video games, immersive media, and generative AI.

## Language Teaching & Tutoring

My earlier teaching experience includes English instruction for Japanese learners and Chinese instruction for Korean learners in China and South Korea. These experiences continue to inform my interests in intercultural communication, multilingual learning, and game-based language education.

- English Tutor for Japanese — Panda Languages, Shanghai, China (Part-time), *2018*
- Chinese Tutor for Korean (Freelancer) — Seoul, Korea, *2019*
- Chinese Tutor for Korean — MandarinSK, Shanghai, China (Part-time), *2020 – 2021*
